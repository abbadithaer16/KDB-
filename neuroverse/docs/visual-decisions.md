# NEUROVERSE — Visual decisions (resolved 26 Oct 2026)

The client delegated the four open decisions from `visual-refinement.md`. Each records the
choice, the reason and the trade-off.

## 1. Violet rim light — yes, desaturated

**Decision:** a desaturated violet rim light separates the brain from the black ground. Fully
saturated violet stays reserved for meaning (selection, the electrical signal, contours).

- Rim: violet hue at about 30% saturation and low intensity (`#8F86B3`-range light colour).
- Meaning: `--violet` `#9C86F0` at full saturation, always with an edge line, never as light.

**Why:** the reference's depth comes largely from coloured rim light; a neutral rim looked flat
in comparison, and a violet cast ties the lighting to the identity.

**Trade-off:** the rule "violet means meaning" becomes "*saturated* violet means meaning".
Mitigated by keeping rim light low enough that selected structures always read as clearly more
violet, and by the edge line and dimming that accompany every selection.

## 2. Scene 2 regions — one at a time; boundaries for the overview

**Decision:** one region is highlighted at a time (violet tint, edge line, rest dimmed). An
"all regions" overview shows lobe boundaries as thin lines with labels, not a colour per lobe.

**Why:** a colour per lobe breaks the single-signal identity, and colour-coded lobes imply
sharper functional borders than the anatomy supports.

**Trade-off:** colour-coded lobes are the familiar textbook convention and read instantly from
a distance. Mitigated by boundary lines, labels, and the presenter stepping through regions
one by one, which suits teaching.

## 3. Scene 5 — the split is the transition; the final frame is fully drawn

**Decision:** a section plane sweeps across the brain, turning tissue into line work behind it.
The final frame is entirely line work on the lit plate.

**Why:** the moment of transformation is the spectacle; ending on the half-and-half split would
copy the reference's composition and leave the ending unresolved.

**Trade-off:** the final frame gives up the realistic tissue the audience has been admiring.
Mitigated by making the sweep the slowest, most deliberate move in the presentation, so the
change itself becomes the closing image.

## 4. Interface — sentence case, no zero-padded counters

**Decision:** "2 of 5" with five short progress ticks; a small "Neuroverse" wordmark in title
case. No "02 / 05", no letter-spaced all-caps wordmark.

**Why:** the reference's interface habits are the generic markers of templated sites and
contradict our type rules.

**Trade-off:** tracked capitals read as "premium" to some viewers. Our premium signal is the
type itself (Newsreader titles, generous space), not the chrome.
